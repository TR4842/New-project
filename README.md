# VocabDB

A comprehensive offline-first Android vocabulary learning application built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, featuring 850 high-frequency vocabulary entries.

## Features

- **850 Vocabulary Words**: Complete dataset including phonetic pronunciations, English definitions, Bengali translations (বাংলা অর্থ), contextual example sentences, synonyms, and antonyms.
- **Search & Quick Alphabet Index**: Instant search across words, English meanings, and Bengali meanings, alongside an A–Z alphabet jump bar.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Built-in audio pronunciation for every word.
- **Interactive Flashcards**: 3D flip card experience to study words and definitions with quick "Still Learning" and "Mastered" sorting.
- **Multiple Choice Quizzes**: Challenge your knowledge with English meaning and Bengali meaning questions, streaks, and score tracking.
- **Learning Progress & Bookmarks**: Track mastery percentage, review sessions, and bookmark favorite words for focused review.
- **Modern Material 3 Design**: Fully responsive, edge-to-edge layout with custom adaptive app icon.
